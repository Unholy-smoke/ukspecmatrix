UK-SPEC · UK standard for professional engineering competence and commitment

Which registration is right for me?

Professional registration is about public trust, professionalism and safe engineering practice. It demonstrates competence, commitment and ethical practice – helping employers, clients and the public have confidence that engineers and technicians meet recognised professional standards.

EngTech, IEng and CEng are different professional registrations recognising different kinds of engineering responsibility and contribution. Every registered professional meets recognised standards of competence, commitment and ethical practice.

Different roles, equal professional value

EngTech, IEng and CEng recognise different types of professional contribution. No registration is “higher” than another. The appropriate registration depends on the nature of your work, responsibility and competence.
